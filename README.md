# Airwire
Daisy Chaining Headphones

Ever been on a bus or train and wanted to show your friend a reel or watch a youtube video together? What are your options for audio?
You can blast the speakers but that's obnoxious and has poor audio quality
maybe hand them one earbud but you only get mono audio and it's kind of unhygenic 

Airwire solves this problem. 
It magnetically attaches to the back of your phone and connects via a USB C cable. each of the 2 removable modules can be handed out to each person and they can plug in wired headphones into them. Audio is then broadcast to each module and output via a 3.5mm jack with high fidelity. Each module also has local volume and mute. When finished, the modules can simply be plugged back into the main case and automatically recharge. 

Airwire utilises the NRF5340 MCU and the BT auracast protocol to transmit audio. It's not limited to just two receivers either; theoretically and practically an infinite of receivers can be connected to the source.
